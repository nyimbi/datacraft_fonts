# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Five families: Datacraft Serif (from STIX Two Text), Datacraft Math (STIX Two Math), Datacraft Sans (Fira Sans), Datacraft Sans Math (Fira Math 0.3.4) and Datacraft Mono (JetBrains Mono).
- Shared vertical metrics across every face: ascender 900, descender -300, line gap 0, `USE_TYPO_METRICS` set.
- Static Regular, Italic, Bold and Bold Italic instances of Serif and Mono for LaTeX and office apps.
- WOFF2 web fonts, `css/datacraft.css` with `.dc-prose` and `.dc-ui`, and the `datacraft` LaTeX package.
- Type specimen and documentation.
