\section{Concentration of a sum of bounded variables}
Let $X_1,\dots,X_n$ be independent with $a_i \le X_i \le b_i$ and $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$.
For every $t > 0$, \textbf{Hoeffding's inequality} gives
\[
	\Pr\bigl[S_n - \mathbb{E} S_n \ge t\bigr]
	\le \exp\!\left(-\frac{2t^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n (b_i - a_i)^2}\right),
	\qquad
	\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}.
\]
